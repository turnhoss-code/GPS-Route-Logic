## 1. Introduction

Welcome to **GPS-Route-Logic** ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a transparent, secure experience when using our Android mobile application and connected automotive services.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, process, and protect your information in compliance with the **Google Play Developer Program Policies**, **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)**, **California Consumer Privacy Act / California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA)**, and the **California Online Privacy Protection Act (CalOPPA)**.

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Location Information (Precise and Approximate)
- **Purpose:** GPS navigation, live turn-by-turn routing, real-time traffic detection, incident bypasses, and multi-profile route calculation (Fastest Highway, Eco-Logic, Scenic, Heavy Cargo).
- **Processing:** Location data is processed primarily on-device in real-time. Background location access is only utilized during active navigation sessions with an ongoing foreground service notification. We do not sell or trade your real-time GPS coordinates.

### B. OBD-II Vehicle Telemetry & Diagnostic Data
- **Purpose:** Scanning Engine Control Units (ECUs), reading Diagnostic Trouble Codes (DTCs), generating AI-assisted repair analysis, and displaying live dashboard telemetry (RPM, vehicle speed, coolant temperature, battery voltage, engine load %).
- **Storage:** Stored locally in an encrypted Room SQLite database on your device and synchronized to your private Firebase account only when you choose to sign in.

### C. Account & Authentication Information
- **Google Sign-In via Credential Manager:** When you sign in, we receive your basic public profile information (Name, Email address, Profile Picture URI, and a unique cryptographic User ID).
- **No Passwords Stored:** Authentication is handled natively through Google Play Services / Credential Manager. We never see or store your Google account password.

### D. Voice Data & Co-Pilot Prompts
- **Speech-to-Text & AI Processing:** Voice co-pilot queries ("Hey GPS", "Hey Route Logic") are transcribed on-device using Android Speech Recognition APIs. The transcribed text prompt is processed through the Google Gemini API to return contextual automotive navigation advice. Audio recordings are not permanently stored on remote servers.

### E. Advertising & Analytics Identifiers
- For free tier users, our third-party advertising partners (Google AdMob) may collect standard advertising identifiers (Google Advertising ID / GAID), IP address, device model, operating system version, and ad interaction metrics to deliver banner and rewarded video ads.
- Paid subscribers (Premium and PRO Max tiers) enjoy a **100% ad-free experience** with ad trackers disabled.
