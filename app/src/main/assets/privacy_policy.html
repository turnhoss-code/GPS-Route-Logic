Privacy Policy for GPS-Route-Logic

Effective Date: August 27, 2026

GPS-Route-Logic processes GPS location data for live turn-by-turn routing and ECU telemetry for vehicle diagnostics. We do not sell your personal data.

1. Data Collection

GPS Location: Used in real-time during active trips to calculate multi-profile routes (Fastest, Eco, Scenic, Heavy Cargo).

OBD-II Diagnostics: Stored locally in on-device SQLite database and synchronized only when logged in.

Google Sign-In: Native authentication via Android Credential Manager.

2. Data Deletion

You can delete all your data at any time via Account & Settings > Delete Account & Wipe All Data or online at Data Deletion Portal.
