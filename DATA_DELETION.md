## 1. Google Play Data Safety Compliance

Under the Google Play Developer Program policies and global data protection laws (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA), all users of **GPS-Route-Logic** have the right to request full, permanent deletion of their account and all associated personal and telemetry data.

You can delete your data through **two easy methods**:
1. **Instant In-App Deletion (1-Click self-serve)**
2. **Online Web Request Portal or Email Submission**

---

## 2. Types of Data Deleted Upon Request

When a data deletion request is fulfilled, all of the following data is permanently purged:
- **Authentication & Profile Data:** Google Sign-In profile, User ID, name, email address, profile picture URI, and subscription quota counters.
- **Vehicle Telemetry & Garage Data:** All saved vehicle profiles, VINs, makes, models, years, and custom mileage logs.
- **OBD-II Diagnostic History:** All recorded ECU diagnostic trouble code scans (DTCs), sensor snapshot freeze-frames, health scores, and AI diagnostic reports.
- **Navigation & GPS Logs:** All saved favorite routes, navigation history, and custom origin/destination waypoints.
- **Voice Assistant Context:** Any cached voice interaction history or transcriptions.

---

## 3. Method 1: In-App Self-Serve Deletion (Instant)

To delete all your data immediately inside the mobile app:
1. Open **GPS-Route-Logic**.
2. Tap on the **Account & Pricing** tab in the bottom navigation bar.
3. Scroll down to the **Data Privacy & Account Control** section.
4. Tap the red button labeled **"Delete Account & Wipe All Data"**.
5. Confirm the warning prompt.
6. The app will instantly wipe the local encrypted SQLite database, clear all cached preferences, sign out of Google Play Credential Manager/Firebase, and reset the app to factory defaults.

---

## 4. Method 2: Web Portal or Email Request (If App is Uninstalled)

If you have uninstalled the app or prefer to submit a remote deletion request:

Send an email to:
- **Email:** support@gpsroutelogic.com
- **Subject:** `Data Deletion Request - com.aistudio.gpsroutelogic.uid`
- **Body:** Please include the email address associated with your Google Sign-In account.

---

## 5. Retention & Processing Timeline

- **Immediate Purge:** Active database records, user identifiers, vehicle records, and scan histories are purged within **24 hours** of request receipt.
- **Backup Deletion:** Automated encrypted database snapshots/backups are permanently overwritten within a maximum retention window of **30 days**.
- **Financial Records:** Anonymized transaction ID logs for subscription invoices may be retained for the minimum statutory period required by tax and financial compliance regulations (Google Play Billing).

---

## 6. Confirmation of Deletion

Once your request has been processed, you will receive a confirmation email sent to your registered address confirming the permanent deletion of your data.
